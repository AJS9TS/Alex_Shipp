    paragraphs:
      - "ADAS features are now standard in most new vehicles. When a windshield is replaced, the cameras and sensors behind it need recalibrating — and if that recalibration isn't done properly, the customer drives away with safety systems that don't work as intended."
      - "When a recalibration fails, we need to know why, and we need to prove to internal audit teams that the customer was told. Before this project, there was splintered reporting and methods of doing this."

  - number: "02"
    heading: "The Solution"
    paragraphs:
      - "I joined the project midway through and we quickly identified gaps in the data sample we were receiving, as well as opportunities to broaden the project's scope."
      - "By engaging with international ADAS subject-matter experts and local data teams, we expanded the data sample that business units sent us, which reduced data quality issues and removed recurring questions about data integrity."

    heading: "Impact"
    list:

      - "Governed and consistent metrics implemented across 8 business units."
